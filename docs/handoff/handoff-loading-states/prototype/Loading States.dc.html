Loading · prim.LogoLoader

The FF mark, waiting with you

The two Fs of the logo take turns rising while the app works. When the work can be counted, like copying files, the mark fills from left to right instead.

Sizes and modes

{{ shimmerStatic }}
Inline · button text
No mark. A shine sweeps across the button's “-ing” label.
Block · 56px
Fills a panel or dialog while that area is busy.
Determinate · 56px
Fills as the work is done. Always show the count.
Splash · 96px with tile
Only while the app itself is starting.

In context. Try them.

1 · Saving a movie
A fast save never shows the loader. A slow one shows it after 300ms and keeps it for at least 500ms.
2 · Deleting a folder
The confirmation dialog becomes the progress view. There's no Cancel, because a half-finished delete is worse than waiting. The dialog can't be dismissed until it's done.

The only full-screen loader. It hands over to the browse skeleton as soon as the server answers.
